# Gali Clans (planned)

Status: planning only. Nothing built yet.

## Social layer
- Miners at level 10 can create a clan. One clan per player.
- Max 10 members per clan.
- The owner can invite anyone. The invited player accepts.
- Anyone can apply to any clan. The owner approves or rejects.
- Members can join without depositing SOL (social only).

## Clan Pool (vault)
- Members deposit SOL into a clan vault, and the vault deploys on ORE's board.
- The vault deploys on all 25 tiles to win a share of the 1 ORE more often.
- Smart skip: the vault sits out rounds where its ORE share is worth less than the SOL it would lose.
- Deposit SOL any time. It joins from the next round.
- Withdraw SOL between rounds only.
- No member claims. The vault claims ORE and pays out once a week.
- Weekly payout: Monday 00:00 MYT. Owner gets 5% of the clan's ORE. The rest goes to members by their share of SOL deployed that week.
- Leaving or kicked: SOL returns right away. ORE comes with the next weekly payout.
- Gali fee: 0.25% on vault deploys for beta testers. May change at public launch.

## Ranking
- Clans rank by ORE mined. Weekly reset Monday 00:00 MYT.
- Reward: bragging rights.

## Clan card
- Shareable card: clan name, tag, emblem, member avatars, ORE mined this week and all-time, clan rank.
- Share to X with a join link.
